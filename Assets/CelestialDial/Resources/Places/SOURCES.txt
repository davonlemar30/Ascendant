The opening's place list and time zones (batch 2, owner Oct 2: the Big Three approved; "Download OK: cities15000.zip from
download.geonames.org (CC BY 4.0) ... Use the IANA tz data already on the Mac").

places.txt: every place in GeoNames' cities15000 (places of 15,000 people or more), 34,152 of them, biggest first. Each line:
the name as listed (a United States place carries its state; two places of the same name in a country carry their coordinates),
its search key when its ASCII name differs from the name, latitude, longitude, and its time zone's line in zones.txt.
Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip, fetched Oct 2, 2026 (3,359,956 bytes, SHA-256
c2fc384e708978317413a86d36886f226a1203dcbf54796416443691f8bba897). Country names from the IANA time zone database's iso3166.tab.
GeoNames data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Credit: place data from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/), CC BY 4.0. Changes: fields trimmed, rows sorted and labelled.

zones.txt: each of the 356 time zones those places use, with its UTC offset in seconds on Jan 1, 1900, then every change to 2040
(minutes since 1900-01-01 00:00 UTC, the new offset in seconds). Worked out with Python's zoneinfo from the IANA time zone database
on this Mac (version 2026c), which is in the public domain.

Rebuild both with Tools/make-places.py from the unzipped cities15000.txt.
