# Celestial Dial solo playtest checklist

This is for you to play the game once, start to finish, and write down what happened. You are the only tester. What you decide at the end is the decision.

Play here: https://davonlemar30.github.io/Ascendant/  ·  Write your decision here: https://app.clickup.com/t/86bbyd0vw

Check a box only if you saw it happen. An empty box means you did not test it. What matters is what your hand did, not whether it sounded fun.

The sections are grouped by what they check, not by play order. Play the whole game once, then go back and check the boxes. Play it again for the "Getting it wrong on purpose" section.

One word to know: the wheel moves in clicks, and each click is one sign. (We sometimes call a click a "detent" or "notch." Same thing.)

## Before you start

This checks that you are set up the way the test expects.

- [ ] Do this on your phone, held upright, using your thumb. Write down which phone and which browser you used: ___
- [ ] Later, on a computer, make the game window small: 360 wide and 800 tall. You should see the whole game with no scrolling. This is only a size check. (How to make the window that size is in Dev notes.)
- [ ] Look at the bottom of the screen before you start. You should see the button say "Reduced motion: off."

## Your first touch of the wheel

This checks what your hand does before anyone explains anything.

- [ ] Do not read anything yet. Just touch the game. Write down what your thumb tried first: drag the wheel, tap a sign, or tap a button: ___
- [ ] Press Next once. You should see the wheel move one sign. Write down if it moved the way you expected, or felt like the wrong way: ___
- [ ] Drag the wheel for the first time. You should feel the same weight as every later drag. It is not heavier the first time.
- [ ] Stop the wheel anywhere. You should see the white bracket around exactly one sign. Nothing is marked right or wrong yet.

## The Earth family, with Caspar's help

This checks that the wheel feels right and that the first family teaches the pattern.

- [ ] Read what Caspar says at the start. You should see him call Taurus a practice sign that stands in for your own.
- [ ] Drag four clicks in one sweep of your thumb. You should feel every click the same. The fourth click is not special.
- [ ] Let go between two signs. You should see the wheel settle on the closest sign, fast, with no bounce.
- [ ] Flick the wheel. You should see it coast one extra click at most, never more.
- [ ] Look at the middle of the wheel. You should see "Framed:" and the sign name. Nothing is sent until you press Keeper's Seal.
- [ ] Press Seal on Virgo. You should see Virgo light up and show "Earth." Then the wheel goes back to Aries by itself, quietly, and moves to Virgo by itself for the next problem, with no clicks counted.
- [ ] Finish with Capricorn. You should see a line join the three Earth signs. You should not see any new astrology words.

## The Fire family, on your own

This checks that you can use the pattern with less help.

- [ ] Start the Fire family. You should see the wheel move to Aries by itself with no clicks counted. Caspar says less. The Count button is still there, and it never tells you the answer.
- [ ] Use only Next and Previous, no dragging. Press Next five times, Previous once, then Seal. You should see it accepted as right, and it counts as your own work.
- [ ] For the next Fire problem, tap the sign you want. You should see it move under the bracket with nothing sent yet. Press Seal. You should see it accepted.
- [ ] Finish the Fire family. You should see six signs lit and six dark, lines on two families, and the Keeper Key message once. You should never see the words "Retained," "Sealed," or "restored."
- [ ] Wait for the offer of one more problem, the Air family. Write down if you wanted to take it, and if you kept turning the wheel when nothing asked you to: ___

## Getting it wrong on purpose

This checks how the game helps when you make mistakes. Start the game over for this part.

- [ ] Press Seal on the wrong sign once. You should see an × by the sign, the wheel staying where you put it, Caspar saying "Not that one. Count your steps again.", and "Help level 1" on the screen.
- [ ] Press Seal wrong again on the same problem. You should see Caspar tell you the rule, "Start at [sign]. Count each sign after it: one, two, three, four.", and "Help level 2" on the screen. Press Count. You should see it still say Help level 2. Flick the wheel. You should see it not coast.
- [ ] Press Seal wrong a third time. You should see Caspar count one out on the wheel for you. Then you get a new problem from a different sign in the same family, and the screen says "Help level 0."
- [ ] Do the same again: three wrong Seals, Caspar shows you one, new sign. Then do it one more time. After the third round, you should see the game stop and say no Key yet. It should not give you a fourth round, even with a new sign.
- [ ] Solve a problem after Caspar told you the rule or showed you one. You should see that answer does not count toward the Key. Only a new problem you solve on your own can count.

## Other ways to play, without dragging

This checks that every way of moving the wheel gets the same answer.

- [ ] On a computer, press the left and right arrow keys. You should see the wheel move one click. Press Tab. You should see it move between the buttons in a sensible order. Press Enter or Space. You should see it press the button you are on. Only Seal sends your answer.
- [ ] Turn on VoiceOver on your iPhone (or TalkBack on Android). You should hear each sign read out with its name, its place from 1 to 12, whether it is framed, and its element if it is lit. Move the wheel. You should hear which sign is framed and the count.
- [ ] Turn "Reduced motion" on and play a problem. You should see the wheel jump to each sign with no sliding and no coasting, and you can still finish.
- [ ] Try every way of moving: drag, Next and Previous, tapping a sign, and the arrow keys. You should see the same answer accepted every way.

## The opening and the ending

This checks the screens before and after the wheel, and the two reward moments.

- [ ] Open the game. You should see a dark screen ask "WHO ARE YOU?" Type a name, or leave it empty. Press Continue. You should see it work either way.
- [ ] Read "Do you know when you were born?" You should see three choices, and Continue turned off until you pick one. Pick one of the first two. You should see a note say chart entry is not in this build and a teaching sign will be used. Pick "I don't know" instead. You should see no note. All three lead to the same teaching sign.
- [ ] Press Continue. You should see a white flash, then the big room: dust, covered furniture, sealed doors, one candle, and Caspar saying hello. Press Continue. You should see the Zodiac Wing.
- [ ] Light the sixth sign. You should see Caspar say "Two of four. The rest will wait for you." Then the wheel splits along its middle and a Keeper Key rises out. Caspar says "Aah... the Library stirs." Write down if the split looked like a reveal or like something breaking: ___
- [ ] Look around after the Key rises. You should see "Keeper Key: 1" at the top, the floor marks brighter, the candle lit, and a Continue button. The extra Air problem is still offered.
- [ ] Press Continue. You should see the big room again, with Caspar telling you to come with him. Press Continue. You should see the Chamber.
- [ ] In the Chamber, you should see seven sealed Books with three locks each, and a dark chandelier. Press "Insert the Key." You should see one lock light up, the chandelier flicker twice and then every candle light, the round mechanism turn a little, Caspar say "So he was right.", and then "End of prototype." Write down if the ending landed: ___
- [ ] Turn "Reduced motion" on and play the whole game again. You should see every moment still finish, with no white flash and no animation.
- [ ] Go through every screen on your phone. You should see no screen that needs scrolling. Do the same in the small window (360 wide, 800 tall).

## Does it fit on the phone?

This checks that nothing is cut off or too small.

- [ ] Look at the wheel screen on your phone. You should see no scrolling, the wheel as the biggest thing, and Seal as the biggest button.
- [ ] Look at the wheel screen in the small window (360 wide, 800 tall). You should still see no scrolling, and the signs and the Count button easy to tap.
- [ ] Look at a lit sign, a framed sign, and a wrong sign. You should be able to tell each apart by words or shapes, not only by color.

## Quick interview

Answer in your own words. Short is fine.

2. Each click is one small step. Did a step feel the right size, too big, or too small? ___
3. The first drag used to be stiffer. Now it is not. Do you miss that? ___
4. When you let go in the middle or flicked, did the wheel land cleanly, or go too far, or stop short? ___
5. After the first round with Caspar, was it clear that pressing Seal is how you give your answer? ___
6. Was the extra Air problem good practice, or a chore? ___

## Dev notes (skip if you're just playing)

- Reference sizes: 390 × 844 logical pixels, and it must fit at 360 × 800. On desktop Chrome, open the device toolbar and set 360 × 800 for the small-window checks.
- Optional event log: the browser console prints lines prefixed `CelestialDial`.
- First-drag resistance is off by default; `DialView.firstDragResistance` re-enables it for comparison. Inertia is `DialView.inertiaEnabled`.
- Known and not a defect: automated pointer input in the very first frame after a phase transition is ignored; not reachable by a human.
- Scripted checks (mechanical, Play Mode, browser) are described in `VALIDATION.md`. They are not this playtest.
