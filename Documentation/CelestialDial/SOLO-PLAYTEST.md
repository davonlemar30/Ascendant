# Celestial Dial solo playtest checklist

This is for you to play the game once, start to finish, and write down what happened. You are the only tester. What you decide at the end is the decision.

Play here: https://davonlemar30.github.io/Ascendant/  ·  Write your decision here: https://app.clickup.com/t/86bbzm11t

This build keeps your progress on your phone. To start fresh, use "Start over (test only)" on the Atrium screen.

Check a box only if you saw it happen. An empty box means you did not test it. What matters is what your hand did, not whether it sounded fun.

The sections are grouped by what they check, not by play order. Play the whole game once, then go back and check the boxes. Play it again for the "Getting it wrong on purpose" section.

One word to know: the wheel moves in clicks, and each click is one sign. (We sometimes call a click a "detent" or "notch." Same thing.) The game uses your own sun sign, so the sign names you see will be yours, not the ones in these examples.

## Before you start

This checks that you are set up the way the test expects.

- [ ] Do this on your phone, held upright, using your thumb. Write down which phone and which browser you used: ___
- [ ] Later, on a computer, make the game window small: 360 wide and 800 tall. You should see the whole game with no scrolling. This is only a size check. (How to make the window that size is in Dev notes.)
- [ ] Look at the bottom of the wheel screen before you start a problem. You should see the button say "Reduced motion: off."

## Your first touch of the wheel

This checks what your hand does before anyone explains anything.

- [ ] Do not read anything yet. Just touch the game. Write down what your thumb tried first: drag the wheel, tap a sign, or tap a button: ___
- [ ] Press Next once. You should see the wheel move one sign. Write down if it moved the way you expected, or felt like the wrong way: ___
- [ ] Drag the wheel for the first time. You should feel the same weight as every later drag. It is not heavier the first time.
- [ ] Stop the wheel anywhere. You should see the white bracket around exactly one sign. Nothing is marked right or wrong yet.

## Your sign's family, with Caspar's help

This checks that the wheel feels right and that the first family teaches the pattern.

- [ ] Read what Caspar says after the wheel wakes. You should see him name your sun sign, say which element it is, and name the three signs in its family.
- [ ] Watch the first problem begin. You should see the wheel show you the count once by itself: it clicks four times, one number for each click (one, two, three, four), then goes back to your sign so you can do it.
- [ ] Drag four clicks in one sweep of your thumb. You should feel every click the same. The fourth click is not special.
- [ ] Let go between two signs. You should see the wheel settle on the closest sign, fast, with no bounce.
- [ ] Flick the wheel. You should see it coast one extra click at most, never more.
- [ ] Look at the middle of the wheel. You should see the name of the sign that is under the bracket, and it changes as the wheel turns. Nothing is sent until you press Seal.
- [ ] Press Seal on the sign four clicks after your sign. You should see it light up and show its element. Then the wheel goes back to Aries by itself, quietly, and moves to that sign by itself for the next problem, with no clicks counted.
- [ ] Finish the third sign of the family. You should see a line join the three signs. Caspar says three signs are joined and names the next element. You should not see any new astrology words.

## The second family, on your own

This checks that you can use the pattern with less help.

- [ ] Start the second family. You should see the wheel move to its first sign by itself with no clicks counted. Caspar says less. The Count button is still there, and it never tells you the answer.
- [ ] Use only Next and Previous, no dragging. Press Next five times, Previous once, then Seal. You should see it accepted as right, and it counts as your own work.
- [ ] For the next problem, tap the sign you want. You should see it move under the bracket with nothing sent yet. Press Seal. You should see it accepted.
- [ ] Finish the second family. You should see six signs lit and six dark, lines on two families, and the line "Two families complete. Two remain." You should never see the words "Retained," "Sealed," or "restored."
- [ ] Wait for the offer of one more problem, a third family. Write down if you wanted to take it, and if you kept turning the wheel when nothing asked you to: ___

## Getting it wrong on purpose

This checks how the game helps when you make mistakes. Start the game over for this part.

- [ ] Press Seal on the wrong sign once. You should see an × by the sign, the wheel staying where you put it, Caspar saying "Not that one. Count your steps again.", and "Help level 1" on the screen.
- [ ] Press Seal wrong again on the same problem. You should see Caspar tell you the rule, "Start at [sign]. Count each sign after it: one, two, three, four.", and "Help level 2" on the screen. The wheel shows you the count once, one click per number, then goes back where you left it. Press Count. You should see it still say Help level 2. Flick the wheel. You should see it not coast.
- [ ] Press Seal wrong a third time. You should see Caspar count one out on the wheel for you, slowly, one click per number. Then you get a new problem from a different sign in the same family, and the screen says "Help level 0."
- [ ] Do the same again: three wrong Seals, Caspar shows you one, new sign. Then do it one more time. After the third round, you should see the game stop and say no Key yet. It should not give you a fourth round, even with a new sign.
- [ ] Solve a problem after Caspar told you the rule or showed you one. You should see that answer does not count toward the Key. Only a new problem you solve on your own can count.

## Other ways to play, without dragging

This checks that every way of moving the wheel gets the same answer.

- [ ] On a computer, press the left and right arrow keys. You should see the wheel move one click. Press Tab. You should see it move between the buttons in a sensible order. Press Enter or Space. You should see it press the button you are on. Only Seal sends your answer.
- [ ] Turn on VoiceOver on your iPhone (or TalkBack on Android). You should hear each sign read out with its name, its place from 1 to 12, whether it is selected, and its element if it is lit. Move the wheel. You should hear which sign is selected and the count.
- [ ] Turn "Reduced motion" on and play a problem. You should see the wheel jump to each sign with no sliding and no coasting, and you can still finish.
- [ ] Try every way of moving: drag, Next and Previous, tapping a sign, and the arrow keys. You should see the same answer accepted every way.

## The opening and the ending

This checks the screens before and after the wheel, and the two reward moments.

- [ ] Open the game. You should see a dark screen ask "WHO ARE YOU?" and nothing else until the game has loaded. Type a name, or leave it empty. Press Continue. You should see it work either way.
- [ ] Read "Do you know when you were born?" You should see three choices, and Continue turned off until you have a sign. Pick the first choice and enter your birth month and day. You should see "Your sun sign is [your sign]." Press "Change my answer" and pick the second choice. You should see twelve signs to pick from. Pick one. You should see "Your sun sign is [that sign]." Change again and pick "I don't know." You should see Caspar say "Then I will choose one for you." and name a sign. Any of the three lets you continue.
- [ ] Press Continue. You should see a white flash, then the big room: dust, covered furniture, sealed doors, one candle. Caspar speaks in a few short pages. Press Continue to turn each page. After the last page, Continue takes you to the Zodiac Wing.
- [ ] Arrive in the Wing. You should see the wheel dark and still, and Caspar say it has been still as long as he can remember. Press Continue. You should see the wheel wake up, sign by sign, on its own.
- [ ] Keep pressing Continue. You should see Caspar say it responds to you, then "It wants you to solve it. Go ahead." Then a short pause with no button, then Caspar's surprise that you do not know astrology, then "But I can teach you. Look here."
- [ ] Light the sixth sign. You should see Caspar say "Two families down. You are halfway through the wheel." Then the wheel splits along its middle and a Keeper Key rises out. Caspar says "Aah... the Library stirs." Write down if the split looked like a reveal or like something breaking: ___
- [ ] Look around after the Key rises. You should see "Keeper Key: 1" at the top, the floor marks brighter, the candle lit, and a Continue button. The extra problem is still offered.
- [ ] Press Continue. You should see the big room again, with Caspar talking about the Key over two pages, then telling you to follow him. Continue takes you to the Chamber.
- [ ] In the Chamber, you should see seven sealed Books with three locks each, and a dark chandelier. Caspar explains the Books over three pages; the "Insert the Key" button stays off until he finishes, then glows. Press it. You should see one lock light up, the chandelier flicker twice and then every candle light, the round mechanism turn a little, Caspar press his palm to a pillar, then "She breathes. After all this time... she breathes.", then "It is faint though. Let us continue, shall we?", then "End of prototype." Write down if the ending landed: ___
- [ ] Turn "Reduced motion" on and play the whole game again. You should see every moment still finish, with no white flash and no animation.
- [ ] Go through every screen on your phone. You should see no screen that needs scrolling. Do the same in the small window (360 wide, 800 tall).

## The return (v0.2)

This checks the loop: coming back to the Library, checking the seals, and finishing the wheel.

- [ ] After "Let us continue, shall we?", press Continue. You should see the Grand Atrium again, changed: one lamp lit, the desk uncovered, the Zodiac Wing door open with light in it, the other doors sealed with locks. Caspar points at the dark seats and mentions the seals. Write down if the room felt changed: ___
- [ ] Look at the bottom of the Atrium. You should see two big buttons, "The Zodiac Wing" and "Check the Seals," and two small test buttons: "Advance one day (test)" and "Start over (test only)."
- [ ] Press "Check the Seals" right away. You should see a note that nothing is due today. Nothing else happens.
- [ ] Press "Advance one day (test)" once. You should see the Seals button say "12 due." Press it. You should see the wheel with your sign already framed and Caspar asking for the next sign in its family. Move and press Seal. You should see "Yes." or the right answer, then the next item.
- [ ] Keep going. You should see the items alternate: some on the wheel, some as a question with four family buttons (Fire, Earth, Air, Water). Get one wrong on purpose. You should see one "Try once more," and if you miss again, the answer and a move on. After six items, you should see "N of 6 seals held." and a button back to the Atrium.
- [ ] Press "The Zodiac Wing." You should see the wheel with six seats lit and Caspar starting the third family from its first sign, with almost no help. Finish it. You should see him hand you the last family with even less. Finish that.
- [ ] When the twelfth seat lights, you should see four family lines, the floor marks fully bright, Caspar say the whole wheel is lit, and no new Key. Press "Back to the Atrium." You should see a second lamp lit and a line that this is the end of prototype v0.2.

## Dev notes (skip if you're just playing)

- Reference sizes: 390 × 844 logical pixels, and it must fit at 360 × 800. On desktop Chrome, open the device toolbar and set 360 × 800 for the small-window checks.
- Optional event log: the browser console prints lines prefixed `CelestialDial`.
- Sun sign from a birth date uses common almanac boundaries (`Zodiac.SunSign`); cusp days can vary by year. Time and place are not used in v0.1. "I don't know" picks a random sign; nothing is saved.
- Count beats: `DialView.BeatSeconds` (0.9 s) and `ReducedBeatSeconds` (0.5 s) pace the Level 2 count and the Level 3 demonstration.
- First-drag resistance is off by default; `DialView.firstDragResistance` re-enables it for comparison. Inertia is `DialView.inertiaEnabled`.
- Known and not a defect: automated pointer input in the very first frame after a phase transition is ignored; not reachable by a human.
- Progress is saved on this device only (browser storage). "Advance one day (test)" shifts the review clock; real reviews come due on the 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 day ladder.
- Scripted checks (mechanical, Play Mode, browser) are described in `VALIDATION.md`. They are not this playtest.
