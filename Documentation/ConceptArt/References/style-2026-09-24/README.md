# Style references, Sept 24, 2026

The controlling style references for the [Ascendant Art Bible](https://app.clickup.com/90141007990/v/dc/2kyd583p-6994/2kyd583p-25174) (ClickUp, owner-approved Sept 24, 2026): a cartoon drawn and lit like a big-budget hand-drawn feature film.

**Style only.** The Scorpio and Capricorn figures are not game characters; do not copy their hair, clothes, or lettering onto the cast. The cast's identities come from their specs (the Keeper: ClickUp Art direction page, Sept 17).

| File | What it teaches |
| --- | --- |
| `ref-scorpio-cafe.webp` | Outlined character and outlined background, flat fills, hard-edged shadows, raking warm light, one dominant hue. |
| `ref-capricorn-doorway.webp` | Backlight and rim light on a flat-coloured figure; an interior drawn with lines and flat planes. |
| `ref-capricorn-turnaround.webp` | Character construction: silhouette, proportions, few shadow shapes. |
| `ref-scorpio-portrait.webp` | Face construction: bold brows, simple eyes with solid pupils, simplified nose and mouth. |
